Happy Birthday, My Love

A letter from the bottom of Bikini Bottom 🌊

✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦

My love,

Happy Birthday ❤️

Three years together… through breakups, through distance, through hard moments and coming back to each other. Our story hasn’t been perfect or easy, but it’s real. And no matter how many times life tested us, we somehow kept finding our way back. That means more to me than I can explain.

But today isn’t just about us. It’s about you — and the incredible woman you are.

I know how hard these past years have been for you. Watching your dad be sick for so long, carrying that worry in your heart every single day, and still standing strong for your family. You didn’t just go through it — you stepped up. You took care of everyone. You became strength when things were falling apart. That’s something not everyone can do.

I need you to know how proud I am of you. Even when you felt weak, you were strong. Even when you felt lost, you kept going. And that’s one of the reasons I admire you so much.

We still haven’t had the chance to meet the way we truly want, and sometimes that hurts. But even with distance, even with the breaks, my respect and feelings for you never disappeared. If anything, seeing everything you’ve handled made me love you in a deeper way.

On your birthday, I don’t just wish you happiness. I wish you relief. I wish you peace after all the stress you’ve carried. I wish you days where you don’t have to be the strong one all the time. I wish you a year where you receive the same love and care you give to everyone else.

You deserve stability. You deserve softness. You deserve someone who sees everything you do and says, “I’m proud of you.” And I am.

No matter what happens in our journey, I will always respect the strong, loving woman you are.

Happy Birthday, my love. 🤍

✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦
With all my heart,
Ayoub
